Wewe

An audio-only baby monitor for Android. Turn one phone into the monitor and another into the receiver, talking directly over WebRTC — no video, no cloud account, no cameras pointed at your baby.

What it is

  • Audio-only, phone-to-phone monitoring over WebRTC
  • Local noise gate — the Monitor only streams when there's sound
  • Cry/noise alerts and push-to-talk on the Parent phone
  • Pairing by QR code, six-digit code, or local-network discovery
  • A convenience signaling relay is provided by default, but everything's self-hostable — see Privacy

Get it

Releases Signed APKs and AABs for every tagged version Source on GitHub GPLv3 · grantstephens/wewe

Privacy

Audio flows directly between the two phones, encrypted end-to-end by WebRTC's own DTLS-SRTP. Read the full privacy policy for exactly what the signaling relay does and does not see.
