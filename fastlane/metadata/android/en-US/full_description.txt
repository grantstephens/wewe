Wewe is an audio-only baby monitor built on WebRTC. Turn one phone into the monitor and another into the receiver — no video, no camera stream pointed at your baby, no cloud account.

The Monitor phone listens on the microphone and only starts streaming when it detects sound, using a local adaptive noise gate — quiet nursery time transmits nothing. The Parent phone plays that audio live, decides whether it's worth an alert, and can talk back with push-to-talk.

Audio flows directly between the two phones, encrypted end-to-end by WebRTC's own DTLS-SRTP. The only server involved is a small signaling relay that helps the two phones find each other and never sees or stores audio — self-host your own (see the project's signal-server) or point at one you trust. There is no default relay baked into the app and no vendor server of ours in the loop at all.

* Audio-only, phone-to-phone monitoring over WebRTC
* Local noise gate — streams only when there's sound
* Cry/noise alerts on the Parent phone (vibration + notification)
* Push-to-talk
* Pairing by QR code, six-digit code, or local-network discovery
* No account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics
* Self-hosted signaling relay — you control it, or trust one you choose
