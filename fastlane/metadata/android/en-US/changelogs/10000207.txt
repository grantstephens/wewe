* New: a dedicated hardware Monitor. Build one on an ESP32-S3 with ESPHome —
  no spare phone needed in the nursery. See the setup guide at
  wewe.hub13.xyz/hardware.
* New: audible alerts. A short beep when a cry is detected, and a repeating
  beep if a Monitor's been unreachable for more than 15 seconds — both
  toggleable in Settings, and mutable per monitor.
* New: a "Listen now" button to check in on the room anytime, even without
  a detected cry — works against a phone or an ESP32 Monitor.
* The persistent notification now shows live connection status (e.g.
  "Monitor connected") instead of a static monitor count.
* New app icon.
* Fixed several reconnect edge cases: push-to-talk no longer goes silent
  after a Monitor restarts mid-call, the app reconnects using the right
  room after a Monitor's pairing code rotates, and the Listen button no
  longer gets stuck showing "on" past its own 60-second limit.
