First beta. Phone-to-phone audio monitoring over WebRTC:

* Pairing by QR code, six-digit code, or local-network discovery
* Local noise gate on the Monitor — streams only when there's sound
* Cry/noise alerts on the Parent phone (vibration + notification)
* Push-to-talk
* Custom signaling relay (self-hosted, no default baked in)
* Foreground-service notification so the mic/socket survive the screen turning off

Known gaps: no TURN fallback for CGNAT networks yet (STUN-only), and this hasn't
been tested across two separate real-world networks yet, only local Wi-Fi.
